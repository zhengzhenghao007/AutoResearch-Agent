# Research direction selection

## 1. Generator-sensitive detector selection (selected)

Compare the highest mean with the highest minimum on a published matrix. The contribution is a reusable decision audit. This is a low-cost secondary analysis, feasible with identified observations. The selected table deliberately includes a failure case.

## 2. Matched encoding and motion

Re-encode matched source clips at controlled quality levels and measure within-clip score changes. This could separate encoding shortcuts from motion cues. Medium cost: it requires licensed clips, detector weights and matched transformations. The causal effect is untested.

## 3. Prospective temporal transfer

Freeze thresholds before collecting newer generator families and independent real sources. Evaluate false positives and calibration by source, with uncertainty clustered by prompt or source video. This design addresses deployment decisions but requires new labels and score-level access. It is the most expensive option and remains untested.
